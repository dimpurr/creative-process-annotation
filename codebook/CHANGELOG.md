# Changelog: codebook

Corrections to the definitions in `schemes.json`, including changes to their source wording.
The definitions are based on the published theories and include brief explanatory notes where needed.

## 2026-08-05

**MaceWard, `realization`.** Replaced with the wording of Mace & Ward (2002) Phase 3 itself.
The previous text came from Botella et al. (2018) Table 1, a paraphrase that added a clause
absent from the original and opposite in emphasis: that a detailed idea of what the artist
is going to do is needed before the stage begins. The original states that making an artwork
does not involve rendering a prethought idea, but is a negotiation between the artist and the
developing work. The other three definitions were checked against the original line by line
and left unchanged.

**Tinio, all three categories.** Replaced with the wording of Tinio (2013) itself. The
previous text came from a handbook chapter summary, which promoted the painting's physical
layers from evidence to definition. The original uses the layers as evidence and defines the
stages conceptually.
