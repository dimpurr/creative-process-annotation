## Tinio  (Tinio (2013))
- initialization: The conceptualization and physical translation of a creative idea and the development of the compositional structure of the artwork. During this first stage the artist explores an idea that he or she considers viable: artists generate a set of potential ideas, one is subsequently selected and initiated by exploring its various facets, and relevant information is gathered through research. As part of that exploration artists construct numerous sketches, initial physical manifestations of an idea, through which the artist uses visual artifacts to embody what has until then been present mainly in the mind.
- expansion_adaptation: Development and adaptation of the initial structure of the artwork. This stage involves the development and fine-tuning of aspects of an artwork that were established during the initialization stage, including continued development of the underdrawing through addition, modification, and deletion of specific elements, and the introduction of such things as shading details. Changes at this stage are relatively small-scale and can be understood as responses to the demands of the evolving work: they are more like modifying already-conceived ideas than introducing something wholesale new.
- finalizing: The conclusion and final refinement of specific details of the artwork. This stage may be considered as beginning when significant structural changes to the foundation of the artwork cease, a sign that the work is nearing completion. What characterises it pertains less to changes to the structural foundation and more to fine-tuned refinements involving color, texture, and other subtle manipulations, together with the specific steps the artist takes to prepare the artwork for presentation.
- none: sentence does not fit any of the above stages. Use freely when uncertain — do not force a label.
